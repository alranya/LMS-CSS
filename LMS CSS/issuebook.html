Counter Guidelines

When issuing books to students at the physical library desk, please ensure:

  • Verify the student's physical College ID Card before confirming the issue.
  • Ensure the book has available physical copies left in the catalog inventory.
  • Standard borrowing duration is 14 days before renewals are required.
